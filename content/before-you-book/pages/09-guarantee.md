## H1: The guarantee — what it covers, and who pays

"Lifetime guarantee" appears on a great many Turkish clinic websites. Read the document
behind the phrase and it frequently excludes the things that actually fail, or requires
you to fund your own return trip to claim it. A guarantee you cannot afford to use is
marketing.

Here is how to read one, and what ours says.

## H2: The eight questions that expose a weak guarantee

**1. What exactly is covered — the ceramic, the implant fixture, or the whole restoration?**
Many guarantees cover only the implant fixture (which rarely fails) and not the crown on
top of it (which is what actually breaks). Manufacturer implant warranties typically
replace the component only, not the surgery or the restoration.

**2. How long, per item?**
Implants, crowns, veneers, bridges and dentures usually carry different periods. A single
"lifetime" number covering all of them is a sign nobody has read it.

**3. Who pays for the revision treatment?**

**4. Who pays for flights and accommodation to receive it?** This is the question that
decides whether the guarantee is usable. If the answer is "the patient", a £300 crown
remake costs you £700.

**5. What voids it?** Standard and reasonable exclusions: trauma, not wearing the night
guard, missing recall appointments, smoking after implant placement, poor hygiene.
Unreasonable: any exclusion so broad that the clinic can always invoke it.

**6. What evidence must you supply?** Typically proof of hygiene visits and recalls. Know
this before you fly home, not after — you cannot retrospectively produce a year of
records.

**7. How is a claim made, and in what timeframe must they respond?**

**8. Is the treatment covered by a third-party medical malpractice insurance, and in
which jurisdiction can you bring a claim?** Turkish clinics operate under Turkish law.
Ask, and get the answer in writing.

## H2: What "lifetime" actually means

Nothing in dentistry lasts a lifetime. Crowns and veneers have a normal service life,
after which they are replaced; implants can integrate for decades but their restorations
do not. A clinic promising a lifetime guarantee on ceramics is either redefining
"lifetime" in the small print or has not thought about it. Be more reassured by a clinic
that says "five years, here is precisely what that includes" than by one that says
"forever" and hands you nothing to read.

### FAQ
**Are Turkish dental guarantees enforceable?** They are contracts under Turkish law. Their
practical value depends on how specific they are and whether the clinic still exists. A
written, itemised document is worth considerably more than a verbal promise.
**Does the guarantee cover flights back?** Usually not — check before you book, because
this is what determines whether you can actually use it.
**What voids a dental guarantee?** Commonly trauma, smoking after implants, missed recall
visits, not wearing a prescribed night guard, and poor oral hygiene.
**What if the clinic closes?** You have no remedy against a closed business. Clinic
longevity is a reasonable thing to weigh.
