## H1: Crowns, veneers, or neither?

If you came to this page because a clinic quoted you 16 or 20 crowns and something felt
wrong, your instinct is probably correct. Read this before you agree to anything.

## H2: The ladder — always start at the bottom

Dental treatment for appearance has a ladder, from reversible to irreversible. Good
practice starts at the lowest rung that solves the problem.

**1. Hygiene and whitening — fully reversible, removes nothing.**
Discoloured teeth that are otherwise sound. Professional cleaning and whitening change
shade with no loss of tooth structure. Many people quoted for 20 crowns needed this.

**2. Composite bonding — minimal or no reduction, repairable, reversible in most cases.**
Chips, small gaps, worn edges, mild shape corrections. Can be repaired chairside and
removed later. Lasts 5–8 years and is then redone — a real limitation, but the teeth
underneath stay intact.

**3. Aligners / orthodontics — removes nothing.**
Crooked teeth. Takes 6–18 months, which is why tourism clinics rarely propose it: it does
not fit in a week. **Straightening crooked teeth with crowns instead of orthodontics is
the defining over-treatment of this industry.** It converts a temporary problem into a
permanent dependency.

**4. Veneers — irreversible, but conservative.**
Thin ceramic bonded to the front surface. Typically 0.3–0.7 mm of enamel is reduced;
some cases are no-prep. Appropriate when shape and colour need changing on teeth that are
structurally sound. Once done, you cannot go back.

**5. Crowns — irreversible, significant.**
The tooth is reduced on all surfaces, often 1.5–2 mm, sometimes 60–70% of the visible
crown. **Legitimate** for heavily filled teeth, root-treated teeth, fractured teeth,
severe wear, or teeth that need substantial structural support. **Not legitimate** for a
sound tooth that is simply the wrong colour or slightly rotated.

## H2: Why crowns get proposed anyway

Not always malice. Crowns are faster than orthodontics, more predictable to fit in a
one-week trip, easier to deliver in volume, produce an immediately dramatic before/after
photo, and pay more per unit. A model built on a seven-day trip has a structural incentive
towards the treatment that fits in seven days. That is a reason to be sceptical of any
plan that fits your annual leave perfectly.

## H2: The questions that settle it

Ask these of any clinic, including us, and get the answers in writing:

1. "How much tooth structure will be removed from each tooth, in millimetres?"
2. "Which of these teeth are sound, and which are already heavily filled or root-treated?"
3. "What would you do if I refused crowns entirely — what is the best result achievable without them?"
4. "Why is orthodontics not appropriate in my case?"
5. "Will any teeth be root-treated, and is that because they need it or to make preparation easier?"
6. "Can I see a mock-up or trial smile before anything irreversible is done?"

Question 6 is the practical safeguard: a wax-up or digital design tried in your mouth,
before preparation. If a clinic will not do a trial, they are asking you to approve the
result after it becomes permanent.

### FAQ
**How much tooth is removed for a crown?** Substantially more than for a veneer — commonly
1.5–2 mm on all surfaces, compared with roughly 0.3–0.7 mm on the front surface for a
veneer. Exact figures depend on the tooth and the material.
**Do veneers ruin your teeth?** They are irreversible but conservative. The problem the
press called "Turkey teeth" is crowns, not veneers.
**Do teeth need root canals before crowns?** Only if the pulp is diseased or the
preparation exposes it. Routine devitalisation of healthy teeth to simplify crown
preparation is not justifiable.
**Can I have crooked teeth fixed without braces?** Visually, yes, with crowns — at the
cost of permanent tooth reduction. Whether that trade is worth it is your decision, and
you should be given it explicitly.
