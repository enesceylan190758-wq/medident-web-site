## H1: Check my Turkey dental quote

Send us the quote you have been given — from any clinic, including ours — and a dentist

No cost, no obligation, and we will tell you if the quote you are holding is a good one.

## H2: Why we do this

Because most of the quotes we see have the same three problems, and the patient has no way
to know. We would rather have the conversation before you pay a deposit somewhere than
after. And frankly: if reviewing our competitors' quotes honestly does not win us work,
the model does not deserve to.

## H2: What we check, item by item

**1. Is the treatment proportionate to the problem?**
The commonest finding. A quote for 16–20 crowns where the presenting complaint was
"my front teeth are discoloured and slightly crooked" is over-treatment. We will tell you
what the conservative alternative would have been and roughly what it costs. See
[crowns vs veneers vs neither](/en/before-you-book/crowns-vs-veneers-what-you-actually-need/).

**2. Is anything irreversible, and was it justified?**
We flag every line item that permanently removes tooth structure or extracts a tooth that
may be restorable.

**3. Is the diagnosis missing?**
Gum treatment, root canals, extractions and bone grafting are the four things most often
absent from a WhatsApp quote and most often added — at extra cost — once you are in the
chair in Istanbul. If your quote has no periodontal assessment line, the number you have
been given is provisional and you should treat it that way.

**4. Are the materials named?**
Implant system by brand and line. Ceramic by brand and type (zirconia, e.max, monolithic
vs layered). "Premium European implant" is not a material specification. Unnamed materials
make like-for-like comparison impossible, which is usually the point.

**5. What is not included?**
Typical omissions: temporary crowns, night guard, CBCT scan, sedation, the second trip's
flights, hotel beyond a fixed number of nights, and revision costs. We list what is
missing so you can ask them directly.

**6. Is the timeline honest?**
We say whether the treatment can actually be delivered in the number of days quoted, or
whether it needs a second trip that has not been mentioned.

**7. Is the guarantee real?**
A guarantee that excludes the most likely failure modes, or that requires you to fly to
Istanbul at your own cost to claim it, is a marketing line. We will tell you which one
you have. See
[guarantee and what happens if something fails](/en/before-you-book/guarantee-and-what-happens-if-something-fails/).

## H2: What you get back

A written response from a named dentist — not a sales message — containing:

- a line-by-line note on the quote you sent
- what we would have proposed instead, and why
- the questions to put back to that clinic, written out so you can copy them
- our own price for the treatment we think you actually need, only if you ask for it

If your existing quote is sound, we will say so and tell you to go ahead with them.
That happens more often than you would expect.

## H2: What we need from you

- The written quote or treatment plan (photo or PDF is fine)
- Any X-rays or OPG you have — a panoramic radiograph makes the review far more useful
- Photos of your teeth: front, upper arch, lower arch, natural light
- One or two lines on what is actually bothering you

Do not send us your passport, ID or payment details. We do not need them and we will not
ask for them at this stage.

## H2: The honest limits of a remote review

This is a document review, not a diagnosis. Nobody — not us, not the clinic that quoted
you — can diagnose from photographs. What we can do is tell you whether the reasoning on
the page is sound, whether anything obvious is missing, and whether the price is inside
the normal range. A definitive plan requires clinical examination and radiographs. Any
clinic that tells you otherwise is the reason this page exists.

### FAQ

**Is it really free?** Yes. There is no charge and no obligation to book with us.

**Will you review a quote from another Istanbul clinic?** Yes — that is the main use.
We also review Antalya, İzmir, Budapest and Albania quotes.

**How long does it take?** Within 48 hours on working days. Complex full-mouth cases
may take longer; we will tell you if so.

**Do you contact the other clinic?** No. Nothing is shared with anyone. Your quote and
images stay with the reviewing dentist.

**What if you think I should not have the treatment at all?** Then we will say that.
See [when you should not come to Turkey](/en/before-you-book/when-you-should-not-come-to-turkey/).
