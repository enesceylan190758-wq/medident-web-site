## H2: Diagnosis

1. What is your diagnosis, and what did you base it on?
2. Will you need X-rays before finalising the plan, and does the plan change if they show something different? *(Expect: yes, and yes.)*
3. Which of my teeth are healthy, and which are already compromised?
4. What is causing the problem I came to you with? *(A plan that treats appearance without naming a cause is a shopping list.)*

## H2: The treatment itself

5. How much tooth structure will be removed, per tooth, in millimetres?
6. What is the least invasive option that would give an acceptable result, and why have you not recommended it?
7. Will any teeth be root-treated or extracted? Why?
8. What is the alternative if I refuse crowns?
9. Will I see a trial smile or mock-up before anything irreversible is done?

## H2: People

10. Which dentist will treat me — full name and registration?
11. What is their specialty and how long have they practised it?
12. Will one dentist do all of it, or several? Who does the surgery, who does the prosthetics?
13. Can I speak to the treating dentist directly before I book? *(Refusal here is disqualifying.)*

## H2: Materials

14. Which implant system — manufacturer and product line?
15. Which ceramic — brand, type, and which lab?
16. Will I receive implant passports and lot documentation on discharge?

## H2: Money

17. Please send the itemised plan with the total, and list explicitly what is **not** included.
18. What are the most common additional costs that arise after arrival, and how much are they?
19. What is the deposit, is it refundable, and under what conditions?

## H2: Afterwards

20. Send me the guarantee document. Who pays for revision treatment, and who pays for travel to receive it?
21. What is the aftercare protocol, who do I contact from my country, and what will my dentist at home receive?

## H2: How to read the answers

- **Speed matters less than specificity.** "Straumann BLX, 4.1 × 10 mm" is an answer. "Premium Swiss implant" is not.
- **A clinic that says "we'll confirm after the X-rays"** is being careful, not evasive. That is a good sign.
- **Any question ignored twice** has been answered.
- **Discomfort at question 6 or 8** is the most informative reaction on this list. A clinician confident in their plan explains why the conservative option falls short. A salesperson gets defensive.

### FAQ
**What if a clinic refuses to answer some of these?** Note which ones. The pattern of
refusals usually maps exactly to where the weakness is.
**Is it rude to send 21 questions?** No. It is a permanent medical decision involving a
few thousand pounds and an international flight.
**Which question matters most?** Number 6 — the least invasive option, and why it was
rejected.
