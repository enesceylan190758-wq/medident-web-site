What is composite bonding?

Composite bonding uses a tooth-coloured resin, sculpted directly onto your teeth to fix chips, gaps and discolouration — usually without drilling or anaesthesia, completed in one appointment. It's reversible, unlike veneers or crowns, which permanently remove tooth enamel.

Composite Bonding vs. Porcelain Veneers

Tooth preparation None or minimal — no drilling Enamel is shaved down (permanent)
